AI Reports are generated automatically using OpenAI's language models. They are not created by a human coach, therapist, or any licensed professional.

NOT PROFESSIONAL ADVICE

OmniV is a self-tracking and personal reflection tool. If you are dealing with mental health concerns, emotional distress, or any medical issue, please speak to a qualified professional.

ACCURACY

AI Reports are based solely on what you enter into OmniV. Their usefulness depends on how completely and honestly you track. Human behavior is complex — AI pattern recognition has real limits. We do not claim that AI Reports are a complete or accurate picture of your psychology or personality. They are tools for reflection, not conclusions.
